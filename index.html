Meet the lily

Elegant petals, a graceful shape, and a fragrance that can fill a room: lilies have charmed gardeners and flower lovers for centuries.

Get to know the lily

A closer look

Grace in every detail

Lilies belong to the genus Lilium, a group of perennial plants grown from bulbs.

Six graceful tepals

Their large, often trumpet-shaped blooms have six petal-like parts and prominent pollen-bearing stamens.

A world of color

From soft pink and sunny yellow to white and deep red, lilies come in many colors and forms.

Sun and good drainage

Most garden lilies thrive in a sunny spot with well-drained soil and a little room to grow.

A flower with meaning

Lilies are often associated with renewal, devotion, and beauty. Their meaning can vary across cultures and occasions.
